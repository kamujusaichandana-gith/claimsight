# Harborlight Mutual Wayfinder Travel Policy

## Coverage

This fictional single-trip policy covers a sudden illness or accidental injury during a scheduled trip, emergency medical transportation, trip cancellation for listed covered reasons, interruption after departure, and baggage delay. Emergency medical expenses are covered up to $75,000 per traveler, and medically necessary evacuation is limited to $150,000 when arranged by the assistance desk. Trip cancellation reimburses prepaid nonrefundable costs up to $8,000 per traveler if a covered event occurs after purchase. Trip interruption pays up to 150% of the insured trip cost for unused arrangements and reasonable return travel. Baggage delay pays up to $600 for essential clothing and toiletries after a 12-hour delay. The scheduled trip must begin and end within 180 days, and coverage applies only to travelers named on the confirmation. Coverage starts at 12:01 a.m. on the day after the plan purchase for cancellation benefits and when the traveler leaves home for other listed benefits. A return journey delayed by a covered event remains insured for up to 48 additional hours. Business equipment and sporting gear are not treated as essential clothing for the baggage-delay payment.

## Exclusions

Known medical conditions that were not stable for 90 days before purchase are excluded from emergency medical and cancellation benefits. Travel against a physician's written advice, losses from ordinary fear of travel, a government travel advisory issued before the policy purchase, and a carrier's financial default are not covered. The policy excludes professional sports, paid hazardous work, and non-emergency treatment sought outside the travel dates. Lost cash, unattended baggage, and items left in a public place without reasonable care are excluded. Trip cancellation for a change of mind or a work schedule conflict is not covered. Quarantine expenses are excluded unless the covered traveler tests positive during the trip and a medical professional orders isolation; the plan then applies the interruption limit. A traveler must disclose a new diagnosis or change in medical stability when purchasing an optional extension. The plan does not cover a missed connection caused by arriving late at the airport without a documented carrier delay. Expenses paid by an airline, lodging host, or credit-card benefit are deducted from any reimbursement for the same loss.

## Deductibles and limits

There is no deductible for trip cancellation or baggage delay. Emergency medical expenses have a $250 deductible per traveler and emergency evacuation has no separate deductible. Medical benefits are limited to $75,000 per traveler, while evacuation is capped at $150,000 and must be coordinated by the assistance desk except when immediate action is needed to prevent serious harm. Cancellation is capped at $8,000 per traveler; interruption cannot exceed 150% of that traveler's insured trip cost. Baggage delay reimbursement is limited to $600 after 12 continuous hours, and the receipt must show a necessary purchase made during the delay. Benefits are secondary to amounts paid by a carrier or another policy. The traveler pays any deductible before eligible emergency medical expenses are reimbursed. The plan does not raise a per-traveler cap because family members share a reservation, and one traveler's unused trip cost cannot be transferred to another. If the airline delivers a bag before the 12-hour delay period ends, purchases made during the wait are not eligible under this benefit.

## How to file a claim

Contact Harborlight Mutual's travel assistance desk as soon as an emergency evacuation or hospital admission is foreseeable, using the number on the confirmation card. Report cancellation or interruption within 20 calendar days after the covered event, and submit the completed claim within 90 days after the scheduled return date. For a delayed bag, obtain a written delay report from the carrier before leaving the airport and keep receipts for essential purchases. Submit claims through the policyholder portal or by the address on the confirmation. Provide the policy certificate number, traveler names, itinerary, event date, and a clear account of the disruption. Do not cancel a trip solely based on a rumor or general concern; obtain documentation of the specific covered event. If the assistance number cannot be reached during an immediate emergency, arrange only the transport needed to reach safety and call as soon as practical. Ask the airline for its case number before leaving the service counter. Keep the original travel booking even if replacement arrangements are made, because the adjuster compares refundable amounts with the new expense.

## Required documents

A cancellation claim requires the original itinerary, proof that the trip was prepaid and nonrefundable, the carrier's cancellation statement, and records explaining the covered event. Medical claims need itemized bills, proof of payment, diagnosis and treatment dates, and the treating clinician's contact information. Evacuation claims require the assistance desk authorization or an explanation of why prior contact was impossible, plus transport invoices. Interruption claims should include unused ticket values and receipts for replacement transportation and lodging. Baggage delay claims need the carrier's property irregularity report, delivery time, baggage tags, and itemized essential-purchase receipts. Submit the requested evidence within 30 calendar days of the adjuster's request; translations must identify the translator. Include a copy of the confirmation showing each insured traveler and the amount paid for that person's trip. For a medical evacuation, provide the dispatch record and the receiving facility's admission note. Receipts should show the purchase date, currency, item description, and total; attach a card statement only if the itemized receipt is unavailable.

## Claim review timeline

The travel team acknowledges a complete claim within three business days. Emergency medical and evacuation claims are triaged within 24 hours when the assistance desk is contacted, although providers may need time to send records. Complete cancellation and interruption claims are usually decided within 15 business days after all documents arrive. Medical bills involving multiple providers or a pre-existing-condition review may take up to 40 calendar days. The adjuster sends a status notice every 15 days if additional review is necessary and identifies missing evidence. Approved reimbursements are issued within seven business days after agreement on the benefit amount. Carrier refunds and other insurance payments are deducted before the final reimbursement is calculated. A claim is considered complete only when the itinerary, loss explanation, and benefit-specific records are available for review. If a carrier's refund decision is pending, the adjuster can review the rest of the file and hold only the affected amount. The decision letter lists the approved benefit, any deduction, and how to request reconsideration.
